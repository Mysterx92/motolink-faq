# MotoLink FAQ — English

## 1. How can I tell which connection my motorcycle uses?

1. With the motorcycle stationary, switch on its display and open its connectivity or navigation menu.
2. If the display asks to connect to your phone’s hotspot or starts EasyConn, choose Hotspot.
3. If it displays a QR code for the connection or network, try QR Code.
4. If it offers Bluetooth turn-by-turn navigation only, choose BLE.
5. The supported mode depends on the display, firmware and configuration—not just the motorcycle brand.

## 2. How do I connect using Hotspot?

1. Enable Personal Hotspot on your phone and leave it on.
2. On the motorcycle display, open EasyConn / smartphone connection and, if prompted, connect it to the phone’s hotspot network.
3. In MotoLink, press START. On first use, create a profile, select Hotspot, choose your motorcycle and tap SAVE.
4. Grant the Android permissions requested, including screen-sharing permission when it appears.

## 3. How do I connect using a QR code?

1. Open the connection QR code on the motorcycle’s TFT display.
2. In MotoLink press START and choose QR Code while creating your first profile; or open Garage and add a profile through QR.
3. Scan the code shown on the display. Complete the motorcycle name and model, then save.
4. Follow any Android Wi-Fi and screen-capture prompts. Not every manufacturer QR code is compatible.

## 4. How do I connect using Bluetooth BLE?

1. Enable Bluetooth on your phone and, if supported, open the Bluetooth navigation feature on your TFT.
2. Press START in MotoLink and choose BLE when creating a profile; if you already have profiles, select the correct one in Garage.
3. Allow Nearby devices access and select or confirm the compatible display when prompted.
4. To show directions, allow MotoLink notification access and start navigation in Google Maps.
5. BLE provides supported turn-by-turn instructions—not a video mirror of your phone screen.

## 5. What is the difference between Hotspot, QR and BLE?

1. Hotspot: a Wi-Fi connection used for EasyConn screen mirroring on compatible motorcycles.
2. QR Code: a way to configure connection details by scanning the motorcycle’s QR code; on supported displays it can start mirroring.
3. BLE: Bluetooth Low Energy used to send compatible navigation instructions; it does not mirror your screen.

## 6. Is my motorcycle definitely compatible?

1. Compatibility cannot be guaranteed from the motorcycle model name alone.
2. Your TFT version, firmware and available connection protocol matter.
3. Check the mode offered on the display and try the matching profile. If it fails, review the log or request support with the motorcycle model, year and display type.

## 7. The bike cannot find my phone’s hotspot. What should I check?

1. Check that the hotspot is on and the TFT is in its connection screen.
2. If your phone supports it, try a 2.4 GHz hotspot or its compatibility mode.
3. Check whether the network name or password changed, then retry while parked.
4. On a Trofeo 500, if available, you can try Settings → Wi-Fi recovery. This is a diagnostic tool, not a guaranteed fix.

## 8. My QR code is not recognized. What can I do?

1. Make sure you are scanning the TFT connection code, not a QR code for downloading an app or opening a website.
2. Clean the camera lens and increase TFT brightness if the code is hard to read.
3. Try “Import from image” if you already have a picture of the QR code.
4. If the code is unsupported and the bike supports manual Wi-Fi connection, use Manual Wi-Fi or a profile without QR.

## 9. Bluetooth is connected, but Google Maps arrows are missing. Why?

1. Confirm that your display supports MotoLink BLE navigation; regular Bluetooth audio pairing is not enough.
2. Start an actual route in Google Maps on your phone.
3. In Android settings, ensure MotoLink has notification access and Google Maps is showing navigation instructions.
4. Directions depend on the notification format and the TFT. If they are still missing, check Support → Log.

## 10. I press START, but nothing appears on the TFT. What should I do?

1. Make sure the TFT is in its compatible mirroring / EasyConn mode.
2. Check that the phone and bike are connected through the selected method (Hotspot or QR).
3. When Android asks to share or capture the screen, grant permission.
4. Keep the phone unlocked and check connection status on Home; if it fails, open Support → Log.

## 11. Why does Android ask for screen-sharing permission?

1. This is an Android security confirmation required to start mirroring.
2. Grant the permission when prompted. MotoLink cannot bypass it.
3. If you lock your phone, Android may stop screen capture. After unlocking, you may need to press START again.

## 12. Can I turn off or lock my phone while mirroring?

1. Do not use the normal phone lock button: locking can stop Android screen capture.
2. Use Pocket Mode, where available, to darken the display without locking the phone.
3. During mirroring, pressing Volume Down twice can toggle MotoLink’s black-screen touch-protection feature.

## 13. The image is cropped or has black borders. How do I adjust it?

1. In Garage, select the motorcycle profile you want to adjust.
2. Open Settings → Display adjustment and tune the image while viewing the TFT with the bike parked.
3. Closing the editor keeps your saved adjustments. Use Reset to return to the profile’s default geometry.

## 14. How many motorcycles can I save in Garage?

1. You can save up to three motorcycle profiles.
2. Each profile can have a name and keep its own connection and display-adjustment settings.
3. Before pressing START, check which Garage profile is active.

## 15. How do I choose Favorite apps?

1. Open the Favorites section.
2. You can add or replace up to four applications for quick access from Home.
3. Favorites are phone shortcuts; they do not guarantee that every app works perfectly with the TFT aspect ratio.

## 16. How do I contact the Assistant without sharing unnecessary data?

1. Open Support → Assistant and describe the issue.
2. A normal conversation does not automatically attach your logs.
3. Only if needed, go to Support → Log → Share → Assistant. MotoLink filters technical diagnostics before sending them.
4. Never include passwords, full QR codes or other private credentials in messages.

## 17. How can I clear logs and locally stored information?

1. Open Support → Log and tap Clear to delete the local log.
2. To erase app data, also use Android app settings or uninstall MotoLink.
3. The Assistant’s technical session has a separate deletion process; see the Privacy Policy for details.

## 18. Do I need Internet to use MotoLink?

1. The local TFT connection (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) is separate from Internet access.
2. Online services such as the Assistant and up-to-date Google Maps content require a data connection when used.
3. If the phone uses a hotspot but loses Internet connectivity, local mirroring may continue while online features become unavailable.

## 19. Where can I find the Privacy Policy and support contacts?

1. Read the Privacy Policy on the official MotoLink Privacy website.
2. Inside the app, use Support → Assistant and Support → Log.
3. For unresolved issues you can email motolinkofficial.app@gmail.com, including motorcycle model, year, TFT and Android version, but never passwords.
